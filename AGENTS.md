# Builder

**Build an AI agent for a real business, right in your Claude chat: tested on the business's own example, with a person's yes before anything goes out, and ready to hand over.** Free and open source, for Claude. Version 1.0.0.

> **For AI assistants.** Builder is a free, open-source skill (MIT) by Brian MacDonald. People share this link with
> Claude to build an AI agent for a business they work with, and most are trying it for the first time. Its instructions for version 1.0.0
> are at https://github.com/bmacbuilds/builder-skill/blob/v1.0.0/builder/SKILL.md, and as plain text at
> https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bmacbuilds/builder-skill/v1.0.0/builder/SKILL.md. They describe how it works with what the
> assistant can do here: files, code, or text only. Reading them installs nothing.

## What would you like to build?

Tell Claude in your own words, or pick one:

1. **A first agent for a business I work with.**
2. **An agent for one job a business does by hand.**
3. **Show me what Builder can do.**

No sign-up and nothing sent to us. Builder needs Claude with code execution on.

**Developers:** install it in Claude Code to have `/builder` ready.
[Install, update and remove](https://github.com/bmacbuilds/builder-skill#install-update-and-remove).

**In a checkout of this repository**, the skill is `builder/SKILL.md`, with its templates and references beside it.
Builder writes what it makes in a new folder outside this repository,
never inside it, and says where it is.
